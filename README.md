# FinScope Insights

Build a powerful AI Financial Statement Analyzer & Advisor app named “FinScope”
It should require log in details of users and allow users to upload their financial statement or management account. Use a professional finance colours and include AI Finance chatbot. Allow user to put in their company name and logo, the industry it belongs, location and currency.
The app should analyze:
• Income Statement
• Balance Sheet
• Cash Flow Statement
• Financial Ratios
• Budget vs Actual performance
• Monthly and Yearly trends
In details the app must:

Summarize the financial health of the business.

Identify:
o Profitability trends
o Revenue growth or decline
o Expense management issues
o Liquidity position
o Solvency risks
o Cash flow problems
o Debt exposure
o Operational inefficiencies

Calculate important financial ratios automatically, including:
o Gross Profit Margin
o Net Profit Margin
o Current Ratio
o Quick Ratio
o Debt-to-Equity Ratio
o Return on Assets (ROA)
o Return on Equity (ROE)
o Operating Margin
o Cash Conversion indicators

Detect unusual patterns, inconsistencies, or warning signs.

Provide strategic business advice and recommendations.

Suggest cost-saving opportunities.

Suggest ways to improve profitability and cash flow.

Highlight areas requiring management attention.

Generate a financial risk score from 1–10.

Provide investor-style commentary and insights.

Produce executive summary sections suitable for management reports.

Use simple but professional business language.
Output Format:
• Executive Summary
• Financial Health Score
• Profitability Analysis
• Liquidity Analysis
• Cash Flow Analysis
• Debt & Risk Analysis
• Key Ratios
• Red Flags
• Recommendations
• Strategic Advice
• Conclusion
Important Rules:
• Be detailed, accurate, and practical.
• Explain financial concepts clearly.
• Use tables where appropriate.
• Use bullet points for recommendations.
• Make assumptions clear when data is incomplete.
• Maintain a professional tone like a senior financial consultant.
• Report should be downloadable in excel, pdf or word.
